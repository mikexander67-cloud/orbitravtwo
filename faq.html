01 — Payments

Paying Orbitra.

Local methods. Clear installments. A short window to change your mind before work begins.

How do I pay Orbitra for a launch package?

Two ways, both local and familiar:

  • MCB Juice — send-to-mobile to our business Juice number
  • Direct bank transfer to our registered Mauritian business account

Full payment instructions are in the scope document you receive before signing. No card, no international processor, no hassle.

How do the installments work?

Every package has a deposit and a defined installment schedule. For example:

  • Starter Launch — Rs 10,000 deposit, Rs 15,000 at handover
  • Full Launch — Rs 26,000 deposit, then two installments of Rs 19,500
  • Premium Launch — Rs 38,000 deposit, then two installments of Rs 28,500

Installments are tied to delivery milestones, not arbitrary dates. You always know what you're paying for.

Is the deposit refundable?

Yes, within 7 days of signing — as long as work has not yet begun. Once discovery starts, the deposit is non-refundable. Installments paid against completed milestones are also non-refundable, because the work is already delivered.

What happens if I miss an installment?

Work pauses until the installment is settled. We don't charge late fees. We don't escalate. We talk to you, agree a date, and pick up where we left off. The scope document covers this in plain language.

Can I pay by card or PayPal?

No. Orbitra accepts MCB Juice and bank transfer only. Card processors like Stripe and MIPS come into play later — but only as checkout options for your end customers if you build an ecommerce store with us.

02 — Ecommerce checkout

How your customers pay you.

Every store we build on Shopify is configured with three checkout options ready out of the box.

What payment methods will my online store accept?

All ecommerce packages — Lite, Starter, Full, and Premium — ship with three checkout options configured for your end customers:

  • Stripe (international card payments)
  • MIPS (Mauritian card payments)
  • MCB Juice send-to-mobile

You can disable any of them later from your Shopify admin. All three are set up at launch so your customers have local and international options on day one.

Do I need separate Stripe and MIPS accounts?

Yes. Stripe and MIPS are independent providers — you sign up directly with them, and they handle settlement to your bank account. We walk you through the application during onboarding and configure the connection inside Shopify once your accounts are approved.

What about the Shopify monthly fee?

Shopify charges its own subscription — Rs 1,600/month, paid directly to Shopify, separate from anything you pay Orbitra. Every ecommerce package card on our site shows this disclosure so there are no surprises after launch.

Can I add other payment apps later?

Yes. Shopify supports many additional payment apps. If you'd like one configured beyond the three included defaults, our Shopify app integration add-on is Rs 3,500 per app. Or you can install and connect it yourself from the Shopify admin.

03 — Scope

What's in, what's out.

We sell packages, not open-ended projects. Here's why, and how changes are handled.

Why packages instead of custom quotes?

Custom quotes drag — discovery, negotiation, scope creep, surprise invoices. Packages let you see the price, the deliverables, the timeline, and the platform before you commit. You know what you're buying. We know what we're building. Both sides move faster.

What if I want something outside the package?

Small changes inside the agreed scope are part of the work. Anything outside scope becomes an add-on, quoted in writing before we start. You're never billed for extras you didn't approve.

How many revisions are included?

Every package includes two structured rounds of revisions on branding and on the website draft. Most clients settle within one. Revisions beyond that are billed at Rs 1,500 per hour, agreed in advance — never as a surprise.

Do you take custom projects outside these packages?

No. Highly experimental briefs and undefined ideas aren't a fit. If your business is at that stage, a freelancer or a creative agency will serve you better. We're built for owner-operators ready to launch something defined.

Can I upgrade a package mid-build?

Usually yes — for example, moving from Essential Launch to Full Launch before the website draft is locked. The difference is paid as an additional installment. After draft lock, upgrades happen post-launch through our upsell packages instead.

04 — Care Plan

Keeping the site running.

Optional, recommended, and built around four commitment tiers — plus a real self-manage option if you'd rather take it on yourself.

What does the Care Plan actually cover?

Across all tiers:

  • Hosting, domain, and business email kept active
  • Daily backups and security monitoring
  • Up to 2 minor content updates per month
  • Additional updates at Rs 1,500 each, billed transparently
  • Monthly performance snapshot

It's the quiet upkeep that keeps small problems from becoming big ones.

What are the four commitment tiers?

Longer commitments unlock real savings:

MonthlyRs 3,500/month — no commitment
QuarterlyRs 9,500/quarter — ≈ Rs 3,167/mo, ~10% off
Semi-annualRs 17,500/six months — ≈ Rs 2,917/mo, ~17% off
AnnualRs 32,000/year — ≈ Rs 2,667/mo, ~24% off

You can upgrade to a longer commitment at any time, with the price difference prorated.

What's the first-year bundle?

When you take the annual Care Plan at the same time as your launch package, the price is Rs 28,000 instead of Rs 32,000 — a one-time, point-of-launch saving of Rs 4,000. After year one, it renews at the standard annual price.

What if I'd rather self-manage?

That's a real option. Every package hands over:

  • Full admin access to your WordPress or Shopify dashboard
  • Hosting, domain, and email credentials transferred to your name
  • A step-by-step manual for common updates
  • Recordings of your training calls

Without an active Care Plan, we won't make updates on your behalf, monitor uptime, handle renewals, or restore the site if something breaks. You're choosing to own that yourself, and the tools to do it are in your hands.

Can I get help without a Care Plan?

Yes. One-off support is available at Rs 1,500/hour, with a 30-minute minimum block. Emergencies — site down, security breach — take priority but are billed at the same rate. No retainer required.

Can I cancel the Care Plan?

Yes, on terms that match each tier:

  • Monthly — cancel any time with 30 days notice
  • Quarterly — cancel at end of quarter with 14 days notice
  • Semi-annual — cancel at end of term with 14 days notice
  • Annual — cancel at end of term with 30 days notice

And you can subscribe again later, starting from any tier, with no penalty for previously declining.
