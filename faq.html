Payments and installments

How do the installments actually work?

Every package has a fixed price and a fixed payment schedule. You pay 40% as a deposit to start. The balance is split into one or two installments matched to delivery milestones — not arbitrary dates.

You see the full schedule on your scope document before you sign. No surprises.

Is paying the deposit safe?

You only pay the deposit after a signed scope document — which lists every deliverable, the timeline, and the payment schedule. Everything is in writing first.

Payments go to a registered Mauritian business account by bank transfer. You receive a receipt for every payment.

What if I miss an installment?

Talk to us before the due date. We'd rather adjust the schedule than have a problem. Work pauses if a payment is more than 14 days overdue, and resumes when it clears.

We've never had to walk away from a launch over a payment. Honesty up front fixes it every time.

Do you offer refunds?

The deposit is non-refundable once work begins — it covers discovery, scoping, and the first build phase. After delivery starts, refunds aren't offered for completed milestones.

If you change your mind before work begins, your deposit is fully refundable.

02

Scope and deliverables

What's not included in a package?

Each package lists exactly what's included. Common things outside scope: extra pages beyond the tier, photography, copywriting for service descriptions, advanced features like booking systems on smaller tiers, and ongoing social posting beyond the setup month.

If you need any of these, we'll quote them as an add-on with a fixed price.

Why fixed packages and not a custom quote?

Custom projects drift. Scope grows, budgets blow out, timelines slip. Fixed packages keep both sides honest — you know what you're getting, we know what we're delivering, and the price is set before we start.

If your business genuinely needs something outside the four launch tiers, we'll tell you during the audit.

Can I change my mind partway through?

Small adjustments — colour tweaks, copy edits, layout preferences — are part of normal feedback. Bigger changes (adding pages, switching tier, changing the offer) are handled with a short change order: we quote the added work, you approve, we adjust the timeline.

This keeps the launch on track instead of stretching it indefinitely.

03

Care Plan and post-launch

Why is the Care Plan important?

Websites need maintenance. Hosting, domain renewals, security patches, backups, email — these things break or expire if no one is watching them. The Care Plan handles all of it for Rs 3,500/month, including up to 2 minor content updates a month.

It's optional. But going without it means you're responsible for everything, and most owners would rather focus on running the business.

What if I don't want the Care Plan?

That's fine. At handover, we transfer everything to your accounts — hosting, domain, email — and you take over. You get the manual, the walkthrough, and WhatsApp support during the support window.

You can also start with the Care Plan and stop later (30 days notice), or skip it and add it later if you change your mind.

What happens after the support window ends?

If you're on the Care Plan, support continues — you can reach us anytime for updates within the included scope. If you're not, you can buy individual updates as needed, or pick up the Care Plan later.

Either way, the site is yours. The files, the accounts, the brand assets — all transferred at handover.
