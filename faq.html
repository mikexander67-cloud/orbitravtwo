Section one

Payments & installments

Fixed packages, fixed prices, paid in defined installments matched to delivery milestones.

How do installments actually work?

Every package has a deposit (around 40%) that starts the work, and the balance is split across one or two installments tied to delivery milestones. For example, Full Launch is Rs 26,000 to begin, then two installments of Rs 19,500 across the build. Nothing hidden, nothing added later.

Is the deposit refundable?

The deposit reserves your delivery slot and covers discovery, planning, and the first build phase. It is not refundable once work has started. If you change your mind before the scope is signed, the deposit is fully refunded.

What happens if I miss an installment?

We pause work, talk to you, and find a solution. Most often we adjust the schedule by a few weeks. We don't add late fees or penalties. If a package can no longer be completed, we hand over everything produced to that point.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer in Mauritian Rupees. You receive a clear invoice for each installment, with the milestone and deliverables noted. No surprises.

Are prices negotiable?

No. Prices are published so every client pays the same for the same package. This is what keeps Orbitra fair and predictable — and how we keep packages tight, fast, and honest.

Section two

Scope & deliverables

Defined packages, defined outputs. Here's what is — and isn't — included.

Why packages instead of a custom quote?

Custom quotes drag on, balloon in price, and rarely launch on time. Packages are faster to scope, faster to build, and cheaper to deliver — and you know exactly what you're getting before you commit.

What if I need something not in a package?

If it's a small addition, we have published add-ons (extra pages, booking system, ecommerce module, etc.) with fixed prices. If it's beyond that, we'll be honest and tell you Orbitra isn't the right fit.

How many revisions do I get?

Each milestone has a defined review round. For branding, two rounds of refinement on the chosen direction. For website pages, one round of content revision and one round of visual refinement. This keeps timelines predictable.

Do you write the content for my website?

We structure your content with you during onboarding — questions, prompts, and a clear template. You provide the raw material; we shape it into pages that read well. For ecommerce, the Full and Premium packages include description support.

Who owns the website and brand assets?

You do. After final payment, all source files, logo files, and access credentials are handed to you. The site lives on your hosting account, under your domain, in your name.

Section three

After launch & Care Plan

What happens once the site is live, and how the Care Plan keeps things running.

What does the Care Plan include?

Rs 3,500/month covers hosting, domain renewal, email, weekly backups, security monitoring, and platform updates — plus up to two minor content updates per month. Additional updates are Rs 1,500 each.

Is the Care Plan required?

No. It's optional and you can opt out at any time. We strongly recommend it because a website left unmaintained will break, get hacked, or lose data. If you decline, we hand over everything and you take it from there.

How long is the Care Plan commitment?

Three months minimum, then month-to-month with 30 days notice to cancel. No long contracts.

What kind of support comes after launch?

Every package includes a handover call and a written manual for how to use your site. WhatsApp support runs for 30 days (Starter, Essential), 60 days (Full), or 90 days (Premium) after handover for any questions.

Can I add new pages or features later?

Yes — through published add-ons. Three extra pages: Rs 8,000. Booking system: Rs 10,000. Ecommerce module: Rs 25,000. Full site refresh after 12 months: Rs 30,000. Same transparent pricing as the launch packages.

Section four

Everything else

Refunds, timelines, working together, and questions we hear often.

What's the refund policy?

Before scope is signed, full deposit refund. After work begins, no refunds — you receive everything produced up to that point. We've never had to make this decision in difficult circumstances, but the policy is clear from day one.

How long does a launch take?

Starter Launch: 30 days. Essential: 45 days. Full: 60 days. Premium: 75 days. Ecommerce ranges from 35 to 90 days depending on the tier. Timelines start the day the scope is signed and deposit received.

What if I don't know which package I need?

That's exactly what the launch audit is for. Five minutes, structured questions, and within 48 hours you receive a recommendation document with the package that fits, the timeline, and the cost. No obligation.

Do you work with businesses outside Mauritius?

Orbitra is built for the Mauritian context — local hosting, local payment gateways, local delivery considerations. We focus here so the work fits where you are. We don't take international clients at this time.

How do we communicate during the build?

Email for documents and decisions. WhatsApp for quick questions. Scheduled calls at each milestone. You always know what stage we're at and what comes next.
