01 — Payments

Paying Orbitra.

Local methods, fixed installments tied to delivery milestones, and a 7-day refundable deposit before work begins.

How do I pay Orbitra?

Two methods, both local:

  • MCB Juice send-to-mobile to Orbitra's business Juice number (primary)
  • Direct bank transfer to Orbitra's registered Mauritian business account

Payment instructions are included in the scope document you receive at signing. No card processors, no foreign payment links.

Can I pay with a card, PayPal, or Stripe?

No. Clients pay Orbitra via MCB Juice or bank transfer only. Stripe and MIPS show up only inside the ecommerce stores we build for clients — as checkout options for their customers, not for paying us.

How do installments work?

Each package has a fixed deposit and a fixed installment schedule tied to delivery milestones. Nothing variable, nothing negotiated.

  • Starter Launch — Rs 10,000 deposit + Rs 15,000 at handover
  • Essential Launch — Rs 16,000 deposit + Rs 24,000 at handover
  • Full Launch — Rs 26,000 deposit + 2 × Rs 19,500
  • Premium Launch — Rs 38,000 deposit + 2 × Rs 28,500

Ecommerce packages run on a 40% deposit + balance over 2 installments matched to delivery milestones.

Is the deposit refundable?

Yes — for 7 days after signing, provided work has not begun. Once discovery starts, the deposit becomes non-refundable. Installments against completed milestones are also non-refundable.

That window exists for a reason: it gives you time to reread the scope, talk it over, and step away if it isn't the right fit.

What happens if I miss an installment?

Work pauses at the current milestone until the installment is settled. No penalty fees, no surprise charges. We'll reach out, agree a date, and resume on the new timeline.

02 — Scope

What's in, what's out.

Packages are defined. That's the point. Here's how scope, changes, and add-ons work in practice.

Why packages instead of custom quotes?

Custom quotes mean variable scope, variable timelines, and variable prices. For most small businesses launching online, that's where projects stall.

Defined packages mean a fixed deliverables list, a fixed price, and a fixed delivery window. You know what you're buying before you sign.

What's included in every launch package?

Every package includes the website build, the branding work at its tier, training calls, a written usage guide, and WhatsApp support for 30 to 90 days after handover (depending on the package).

You also get full admin access and credentials in your name at handover. The site is yours.

What's not included?
  • Unlimited revisions or open-ended creative direction
  • Ongoing content writing, photography, or post production (outside what each tier specifies)
  • Custom one-off projects outside the defined packages
  • Paid ad spend (managed separately under Ads Management)

If you need something beyond the package, we'll quote it as a clearly priced add-on or upsell — never as a vague extra.

Can I change scope mid-project?

Small adjustments inside the original scope — fine, we handle them as we go. Larger changes (extra pages, new modules, switching package tier) get quoted as an add-on so the price and timeline stay clear.

Common upsells: 3 extra pages for Rs 8,000, booking system for Rs 10,000, ecommerce module for Rs 25,000.

Who owns the site and the brand assets?

You do. Domain, hosting account, admin credentials, brand files, source assets — all transferred to your name at handover. You can self-manage from day one if you choose to.

03 — Ecommerce checkout

How your store accepts payments.

Three checkout options configured for your customers. Plus the ongoing Shopify subscription, disclosed openly.

What payment methods will my store accept?

Every ecommerce package — Lite, Starter, Full, Premium — comes with three checkout options configured for your end-customers:

  • Stripe (international cards)
  • MIPS (local Mauritian cards)
  • MCB Juice send-to-mobile

Your customers pick the one they prefer at checkout. You receive funds into your own connected accounts.

What does Shopify cost on top of the package?

Shopify charges its own monthly subscription of Rs 1,600/month, paid directly to Shopify. That's separate from the launch package and separate from the Care Plan.

If you also take the Care Plan, your total ongoing monthly is roughly Rs 4,267 to Rs 5,100 depending on which commitment tier you pick.

What if I have more than 10 products?

Step up to the next tier: Ecommerce Starter handles 11–30 products, Full handles 31–100, Premium handles 100+ or complex variants.

If you're close to the edge, smaller add-ons exist too — extra 25 products beyond a tier limit is Rs 4,000, and an Ecommerce Lite to Starter migration is Rs 35,000.

Do you write product descriptions or take photos?

Ecommerce Full includes either description writing OR photo optimisation (your pick). Ecommerce Premium includes both as a workflow.

On Lite and Starter, you supply finished product photos and copy. We can quote add-ons: photography session at Rs 8,000, description writing at Rs 5,000 per 25 products.

What's included in the Care Plan?
  • Hosting, domain, and business email kept active
  • Daily backups and security monitoring
  • Up to 2 minor content updates per month
  • Additional updates at Rs 1,500 each, billed transparently
  • Monthly performance snapshot
Can I cancel or change tiers?
  • Monthly: cancel any time with 30 days notice
  • Quarterly: cancel at end of quarter with 14 days notice
  • Semi-annual: cancel at end of term with 14 days notice
  • Annual: cancel at end of term with 30 days notice

You can upgrade to a longer commitment at any time — the price difference is prorated.

What if I'd rather self-manage?

Self-management is a real option. Every package hands over full admin access, hosting and domain credentials in your name, a step-by-step manual, and recordings of training calls.

Without an active Care Plan, Orbitra won't make updates on your behalf, monitor for downtime, handle renewals, or restore the site if something breaks. That's the trade-off — and it's a fair one if you're comfortable running things yourself.

Can I get help without subscribing?

Yes — one-off support sessions are Rs 1,500/hour, 30-minute minimum block. Emergencies (site down, security breach) take priority but bill at the same rate.

You can also subscribe to the Care Plan at any later point, starting from any tier. No penalty for previously declining.
