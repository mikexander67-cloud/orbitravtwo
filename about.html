Philosophy

How we think about launching.

We don't sell custom projects. We sell launch readiness — the website, the brand, the social presence, and the knowledge to operate them. As one defined package, paid in installments.

Packages, not quotes.

Open-ended quotes drift. Packages don't. You know the price, the timeline, and what's included before you sign. Scope changes are priced, not absorbed.

Installments, not invoices in the dark.

40% deposit, balance against milestones. Paid via MCB Juice or bank transfer — the way you already pay everything else.

Handover with the keys.

Every launch ships with admin access, a custom manual, and recorded training calls. Self-manage if you want. Subscribe to Care if you'd rather not.

Education, not mystery.

You should understand what you bought and how it works. We build the foundation, then we teach you how to stand on it.

Who we work with

A good fit, and an honest "not yet."

We'd rather tell you we're not the right partner than take a deposit we can't honour.

A good fit

  • Early-stage Mauritian businesses launching their first proper digital presence.
  • Service operators, owner-operators, small product brands.
  • Owners who value clarity over endless options.
  • Ready to pay a deposit and follow a structured 30–75 day build.
  • Want to learn how to operate what they buy.

Probably not us

  • Looking for a fully custom, one-off creative project.
  • Highly experimental briefs without a defined business behind them.
  • Expecting unlimited revisions or open-ended creative direction.
  • Unable to pay the deposit upfront.
  • Need same-week turnaround or skip-the-process delivery.

If you're somewhere in between, start the audit anyway. We'll tell you honestly whether a package fits — or point you somewhere better.
