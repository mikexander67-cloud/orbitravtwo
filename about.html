About Orbitra

A launch agency built for the businesses other agencies overlook.

Orbitra sits between the freelancer and the full agency. We sell launch readiness — the website, the brand, the social presence, and the literacy to operate them — as defined packages with installments, not open-ended quotes.

Our positioning

Fixed packages, transparent prices, and payments that match the work.

Why we built it this way.

Small Mauritian businesses keep getting two bad options. A freelancer who delivers cheaply but vanishes after handover. A full agency that quotes in lakhs and disappears behind a project manager.

Neither fits the owner-operator launching their first real digital presence. So we built a third option — structured, supported, and priced to be reachable. You see the package, you see the price, you pay in installments matched to milestones, and you leave with the literacy to run what we built.

Defined scope

Eight packages. Published prices. No custom proposals.

Local payments

MCB Juice or bank transfer. No card, no hassle.

Real handover

Training calls and a custom manual, every package.

Who we work with

A good fit looks like this.

We work best with people who want clarity over options, and who are ready to invest in a proper start.

We're a fit if you…

  • Are launching your first real digital presence — or relaunching one that never worked.
  • Run a service business, small product brand, or owner-operator shop in Mauritius.
  • Want a fixed package with a deposit and installments, not an open-ended quote.
  • Need handholding and education, not just files delivered to a folder.
  • Prefer to pay via MCB Juice or bank transfer.

We're not a fit if you…

  • Need a highly experimental or bespoke creative brief outside our packages.
  • Haven't yet defined what your business actually offers.
  • Can't pay the deposit upfront before work begins.
  • Want unlimited revisions or open-ended creative direction.
  • Are shopping for the cheapest possible option, regardless of structure.
A note on honesty

If we're not the right fit, we'll say so.

The audit isn't a sales funnel. If your business needs something we don't offer — a fully custom build, a high-end brand consultancy, an enterprise platform — we'll tell you, and where possible point you toward someone who does that work well.

Clarity is the product. That includes being clear when the answer is no.
