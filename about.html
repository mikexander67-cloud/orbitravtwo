Positioning

What we actually are.

A structured launch agency for early-stage and growing Mauritian businesses.

Most small businesses in Mauritius are stuck between two bad choices. Freelancers are affordable, but the work is ad-hoc — you get a website, but no system, no plan, no follow-through. Full agencies are structured, but the price tag is out of reach for a business still finding its footing.

Orbitra is the middle path. We sell launch readiness — the website, the brand, the social setup, and the literacy to operate them. All as one defined package, with installments over 2–3 months.

We work with service operators, small product brands, and owner-operators launching their first real digital presence. We don't take on undefined briefs, experimental creative direction, or clients who can't commit to a deposit. That's not gatekeeping — it's how we keep delivery honest.

How we work

Five principles we hold to.

The quiet rules behind every Orbitra launch.

I.

Clarity over options.

Most buyers don't want twenty choices. They want the right one. We narrow the field to a handful of packages, recommend the fit, and let you decide.

II.

Education is part of the deliverable.

Every launch includes calls, a written manual, and WhatsApp support. You finish knowing how to use what we built — not staring at a dashboard you don't understand.

III.

Transparency on ongoing costs.

Hosting, domains, Shopify fees, Care Plan — all disclosed before you sign. No hidden recurring charges showing up two months in.

IV.

Scope changes have a price.

If the brief grows, the package grows with it — at a published rate. We don't fold extras in quietly, and we don't argue about what was promised.

V.

Built for where you are now.

Starter Launch isn't a lesser Full Launch — it's the right shape for a business with one focused offer. We size the package to the stage, not the ambition.

The Care Plan

Why we built it in.

A website without maintenance is a website that breaks. Hosting expires. Domains lapse. Backups go stale. Security patches go unapplied. We see it constantly — beautiful launches that quietly fail six months later because nobody is minding the back end.

The Care Plan is optional, but we recommend it for every launch client. Rs 3,500/month covers hosting, domain, email, backups, security, monitoring, and up to two minor content updates. Three-month minimum, then month-to-month. You can leave anytime with 30 days notice.

It's there so you can focus on running your business — not maintaining your website.
