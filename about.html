Our philosophy

A calmer way to launch.

Most small businesses in Mauritius hit the same wall: freelancers are cheap but unstructured, and full agencies are out of reach. Quotes vary wildly. Scopes shift mid-project. Nobody explains what happens after handover.

Orbitra exists to remove that guesswork. Every launch is a defined package. Every package has a fixed price, a fixed timeline, and a clear list of what's included. Payments are split over 2–3 months. And every client learns how to actually use what we build.

We don't sell custom projects. We sell launches that finish on time.
Why packages

Three options on the market. We chose a fourth.

Here's how Orbitra sits between the alternatives most small businesses face.

Freelancers

Cheap but ad-hoc

Low cost, low structure. Scopes drift. Timelines slip. No training, no plan for what happens after the site is delivered.

Orbitra

Fixed packages, installments

Defined deliverables. Defined timeline. Payments over 2–3 months. Every client gets training and a manual. The work finishes.

Full agencies

Polished but unreachable

Strong work, but priced for established companies with retainers. Too expensive and too long for a business launching its first digital presence.

Who it's for

Honest about fit.

Orbitra works well for some businesses, and not for others. Better to say so upfront.

A good fit

  • Early-stage or growing Mauritian businesses
  • Service operators, small product brands, owner-operators
  • First real digital presence, or replacing something improvised
  • Willing to invest if structure and price are clear
  • Open to learning how to run what we build

Probably not a fit

  • Highly experimental or undefined business ideas
  • Clients seeking unlimited revisions or bespoke creative direction
  • One-off custom projects outside our defined packages
  • Unable to pay the deposit upfront
  • Looking for the cheapest possible option
How we work

Principles that keep launches on track.

Defined scope

Every package lists exactly what's included and what's not. No mid-project surprises, no expanding briefs, no quiet cost creep.

Fixed timelines

30, 45, 60, or 75 days depending on the package. Milestones are tied to payments so progress stays visible on both sides.

Published pricing

Every price is on the site. Nothing is negotiated case by case. You see the same numbers everyone else sees.

Training included

Calls, a custom manual, and WhatsApp support. We hand you a launch you can actually run, not a tool you can't open.

Care after launch

The optional Care Plan keeps your site running — hosting, backups, security, monitoring, minor updates. Calm operations, not chaos.

Built for Mauritius

Local hosting partners, local payment gateways, local context. We know who you're selling to because we live here too.
